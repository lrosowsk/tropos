Questions

Good to know.

Do I need to take everything the survey recommends?

No. The survey ranks products by how well they match your answers. Start with the top one or two and add more if you want. Each recommendation shows the reason it was chosen.

How does the subscription work?

A monthly subscription is 15% off. Prepay 3 months for 20% off or a year for 40% off, still delivered monthly. Shipping is free on subscriptions of $100 or more a month, and your 2nd delivery includes a free weekly pill organizer. You can skip, swap products, pause or cancel any time from your account, and there's no minimum number of orders.

How are your products tested?

Each batch is tested by an independent, ISO 17025-accredited lab for identity, potency, microbes and heavy metals. Results are matched to the lot number printed on your bottle.

Can I take these with medication?

Some ingredients, like ashwagandha, vitamin K2 and caffeine, can interact with medications or health conditions. Each product lists its cautions. If you take prescription medication, check with your doctor first.

When does Tropos ship?

We're launching soon. Reserve your stack now and it's held at today's price. We'll email you before anything ships, and you won't be charged until you confirm.

How much is shipping?

Shipping is $6.95 per delivery on one-time orders and on subscriptions under $100 a month. Once your subscriptions add up to $100 or more a month, shipping is free on every delivery.
